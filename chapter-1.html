Chapter One

The Girl I Knew Before the Struggle

Before addiction became part of her story, there was a girl I knew, a family I watched, and a woman who was always searching for God.

She is my cousin, but that word does not fully describe why her story is so important to me. I have known her for most of my life, and because she is older than all of her brothers and sisters, I had the opportunity to watch her grow up before I watched many of them find their own way in the world.

I saw her before addiction became part of her story. I saw the person she was long before anyone could define her by the things she struggled with.

That is why I have always found it difficult to look at her life and see only addiction. I know there is so much more to her than that.

I have also watched some of her brothers and sisters walk into places that looked much like the places she had once been. Some are still there. Some may not even realize that they have begun walking the same road.

That makes her story even more meaningful to me, because I have seen enough of our family to know that addiction does not always announce itself when it arrives. Sometimes it slowly becomes a part of a person’s life until they can no longer see how much control it has taken.

Before the struggle

There was a person here before there was a problem.

She was raised in the Baptist faith, and that was the faith she knew. She believed what she had been taught, attended church, and had experiences where people worshipped, prayed, cried out, and sought after God.

She was always something of a free spirit, though. There was something in her that seemed to reach beyond what she could explain.

She wanted to feel God.

She wanted to know that He was real.

There were times when she cried out in church, sometimes from fear and sometimes from desperation. Looking back now, I wonder if some of those cries were coming from a place inside her that already knew she needed something deeper than what she had found.

She was searching for relief, although she probably could not have explained exactly what she was searching for.

The heart beneath the struggle

She was capable of surrender.

When she believed in something, she could give herself to it.

When something captured her attention, she could become deeply invested in it.

She did not know it at the time, but there was something about the way she was made that would eventually become central to her story. She was capable of surrender.

When she believed in something, she could give herself to it. When something captured her attention, she could become deeply invested in it.

What I understand now

She had the heart of a worshipper.

She simply had not yet discovered the One who deserved that worship.
