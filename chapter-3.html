CHAPTER THREE

The God She Finally Found

The search for relief became a journey toward redemption.

For years, she was searching for something that could bring relief to the places in her life that hurt. She searched in places that could never truly satisfy her, and eventually she had to face what those things had been doing to her.

But God had not abandoned her during those years.

He had been there.

Even when she did not recognize Him, He knew exactly where she was.

As she grew older, something began to change in the way she understood God. The faith she had grown up with was no longer the only way she understood her relationship with Him. She began embracing the apostolic doctrine she now believes, and her faith became something personal rather than simply something she had inherited.

A PERSONAL FAITH

The God she had cried out to in fear and desperation began to become real to her in a different way.

She began to understand that the presence of God she had been searching for was not something she had to chase through the things of this world. The relief she had been looking for could not be found in addiction. It could not be found in anything that demanded her life in exchange for a temporary escape.

What she needed was God.

And when she began turning toward Him, something that had been broken for a long time began to change.

SURRENDER

She did not lose her ability to surrender.

She found the right place to surrender it.

That is what I love most about her story.

God did not have to turn her into someone completely different. He had to turn her heart toward the One who could receive what she had been giving away to the wrong things.

WHAT CHANGED

The woman who had once been captivated by something that was destroying her discovered that she could be captivated by God instead.

The woman who had spent years trying to escape her pain discovered that she could bring that pain to Him.

The woman who had once been held by something that wanted to destroy her found herself held by the God who wanted to restore her.

And that is why I cannot look at her life and simply call it a story about addiction.

It is a story about a woman who was searching.

It is a story about a woman who learned how to surrender.

It is a story about a family that has seen both the devastation of addiction and the possibility of freedom.

THE GRACE OF GOD

Most of all, it is a story about a God who could look beyond the addiction and see the person He had created.

She may not have understood how much God loved her while she was in the middle of her struggle. She may not have understood why she could surrender so easily to something that was hurting her. She may not have understood why she could become so deeply attached to something that was taking her life.

But God understood.

THE WORSHIPPER

He knew there was a worshipper underneath it all.

And He knew that if He could turn her around, she would not simply walk away from what had held her.

She would walk toward Him.

She did.

And now the story is different.

The thing that once held her captive no longer gets to define her.

She is alive.

She is free.

She is still a worshipper.

But now she knows who deserves her worship.

She belongs to God.
