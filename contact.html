A NOTE TO READERS

Every message from a reader is meaningful.

This story was written because I believe people are more than the worst things they have experienced or the worst decisions they have made.

If you have walked through addiction yourself, loved someone who has, or simply understand what it means to need grace, your story matters too.

Thank you for reading.

Thank you for listening.

Thank you for believing that stories can change.

IMPORTANT

This website and book are not intended to replace professional medical, mental health, addiction treatment, or emergency services.

If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, contact emergency services or an appropriate crisis or treatment resource.

Thank you for entering this story.

May you find grace in the places you least expect it.

And may you remember that the story is not finished.
