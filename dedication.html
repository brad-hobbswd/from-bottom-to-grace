FOR THOSE WHO NEED GRACE

Dedication

For the ones who are still searching, still fighting, and still finding their way home.

This book is dedicated to those who have known what it means to fall, to struggle, to search, and to wonder whether there is still a way back.

To those who have struggled with addiction.

To those who have loved someone struggling with addiction.

To the families who have cried, prayed, waited, hoped, and wondered what would happen next.

To the people who have been defined by their worst moments.

To those who have been told, directly or indirectly, that they are too far gone.

You are more than what happened to you.

Your struggle is not the end of your story.

This book is dedicated to the ones who are still searching for something that will satisfy the deepest places of their hearts.

May you discover that what you have been searching for cannot be found in the things that promise temporary relief.

May you discover grace.

May you discover mercy.

May you discover restoration.

And most of all, may you discover the God who never stopped looking for you.

To my cousin, whose story reminded me that addiction may become part of someone's story, but it does not have to become the ending.

Your life is more than what you survived.

You are still here.

You are still a worshipper.

And your story is still being written.

Bradley Hobbs
