BEFORE THE STORY

Preface

Some stories are written because they are easy to tell. Others are written because they are necessary.

There are some stories that are difficult to tell because they are deeply personal. This is one of those stories.

From the Bottom to Grace was not written simply to tell the story of addiction. It was written because I believe there is a person behind every struggle, a heart behind every mistake, and a story behind every label.

When someone has struggled with addiction, it can become easy for everyone around them to see only the addiction.

But that is never the whole story.

LOOKING BEYOND THE STRUGGLE

Before addiction became part of her story, she was a daughter, a sister, a cousin, and a person with hopes, questions, faith, fears, and dreams.

I knew her before the struggle.

I watched her grow up. I watched our family grow and change. I saw some people make choices that led them into difficult places, and I have watched others remain in those places.

That perspective matters to me because addiction does not happen in isolation. It affects families. It affects relationships. It affects the people who are watching someone they love struggle to find their way back.

WHAT I BELIEVE

A person's worst chapter does not have to become their entire story.

That is one of the reasons I wanted to write this book.

I wanted to remember the person beneath the struggle.

I wanted to acknowledge the damage addiction can cause without allowing addiction to have the final word.

And I wanted to tell the story of what happens when a person who has spent years searching for relief finally discovers where that searching needs to lead.

GRACE

Grace does not erase the past. Grace changes what the past gets to mean.

There were years when she searched for something that could quiet the pain inside her. There were things she surrendered herself to that could never give her what she truly needed.

But God saw something different.

He saw a worshipper.

He saw the person underneath the addiction.

He saw what she could become when her heart was turned toward Him.

She did not have to become someone completely different.

She had to discover the One worthy of her surrender.

That is the heart of this book.

It is not a story intended to glorify addiction.

It is not a story intended to hide the consequences of addiction.

It is a story about redemption.

It is a story about grace.

It is a story about what God can do with a life that once seemed completely lost.

If you are reading this because addiction has touched your life, I hope you see more than the darkness.

If you are reading because someone you love is struggling, I hope you remember that their struggle is not the whole person.

And if you are reading because you need to know whether grace can reach someone at the bottom, I hope these pages give you reason to believe that it can.

The story is not about how far she fell.

The story is about the God who was still there when she turned around.
