The Story Behind the Book

Some stories are not about where someone fell.

They are about the grace that met them there.

From the Bottom to Grace tells a deeply personal story of addiction, surrender, redemption, faith, and unconditional love.

Told through the eyes of a cousin who witnessed the journey, this is a story about seeing the person beneath the struggle and believing that a life can become something more than the place it once seemed to be.

“ She did not stop being a worshipper. She found the One worthy of her worship.

Why This Story Matters

Because a person's worst chapter is not always their final one.

Addiction can change the way people see themselves. It can change the way families see them. It can make hope feel distant.

But grace tells a different story.

There can still be something ahead.
