A QUIET PLACE

Prayer Room

Come as you are. Bring the things you cannot carry alone.

This is a place to slow down.

A place to breathe.

A place to bring the things that are difficult to say anywhere else.

You do not have to have the right words. You do not have to have everything figured out. You simply have to come.

A PRAYER FOR THE ONE WHO IS STRUGGLING

God, meet me in the place where I am.

You know the things I have tried to hide.

You know the pain I have carried.

You know the things I have surrendered myself to that were never meant to have control over me.

Help me understand that my struggle does not make me worthless.

Give me the courage to turn around.

Give me the strength to take the next step.

Teach me to surrender my life to You instead of surrendering it to the things that destroy me.

I am still here.

My story is not finished.

I choose to turn toward You.

Help me receive Your grace one day at a time.

Help me believe that restoration is possible.

Help me become the person You created me to be.

Let what once held me captive lose its power over me.

Let Your grace become greater than my past.

Amen.

A PRAYER FOR THE FAMILY

God, remember the families who are hurting.

Be near to the mother who does not know what to do.

Be near to the father who is carrying more than he says.

Be near to the brother or sister who keeps hoping for change.

Be near to the person who has cried out for someone they love.

Give families wisdom to know when to help, when to wait, when to speak, and when to simply remain present.

Protect them from losing hope.

Remind them that they cannot control another person's choices, but they can continue to love, pray, and believe that change is possible.

Give them grace for the journey.

Give them strength for today.

Amen.

A PRAYER FOR THOSE FINDING FREEDOM

God, strengthen the person who has decided to begin again.

Give them courage when the road feels difficult.

Give them people who will encourage them rather than define them by their past.

Give them wisdom to recognize the things that can pull them backward.

Give them the strength to keep moving forward.

When shame tells them they are still the person they used to be, remind them that their story has changed.

What once defined them does not have to define them forever.

Help them walk in freedom.

Help them walk in truth.

Help them walk toward You.

Amen.

A MOMENT OF SILENCE

Before you leave this page, take a moment.

Think about the person you are praying for.

Think about the part of your own life that you have been afraid to surrender.

Think about the thing you have carried for too long.

You do not have to solve everything right now.

Just bring it to God.

There is no perfect way to pray.

There is only the decision to turn toward God.

Come as you are.

Bring what hurts.

Bring what you cannot fix.

Bring your whole heart.

REMEMBER

The story is not finished.

Grace is still possible.
