ABOUT THE BOOK

This is not simply a story about addiction.

It is a story about a woman who was searching for relief, a family that witnessed the effects of addiction, and a God who never stopped seeing the person underneath the struggle.

It is about the difference between surrendering to something that destroys and surrendering to the One who restores.

It is about grace that reaches into places where people may believe there is no hope left.

Addiction does not get the final word.

Grace does.

FOR THE READER

Whether you have experienced addiction yourself, loved someone who has struggled, or simply need a reminder that a person's worst season does not have to define them, this book was written with you in mind.

There is more to your story than where you have been.

Grace can still write what comes next.

This book is a story of redemption, but it is also an invitation to look again.

Look beyond the label.

Look beyond the struggle.

Look for the person.

Look for grace.
