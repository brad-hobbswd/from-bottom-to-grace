WITH GRATITUDE

Acknowledgments

No story is written alone.

Every story carries the fingerprints of people who were there along the way.

Some people help us through their words. Some through their prayers. Some through their presence. Others teach us simply by allowing us to witness what their lives have endured and overcome.

This book exists because of those people.

TO MY FAMILY

To my family, thank you for being part of the story even when the story was difficult.

Families experience addiction in ways that are often difficult to explain to people outside of them. There are moments of fear, disappointment, confusion, anger, prayer, hope, and waiting.

Thank you to the people who continued to love through those moments.

Thank you to those who kept hoping when hope felt difficult.

Thank you to those who understood that loving someone does not mean pretending the struggle does not exist.

TO MY COUSIN

Thank you for allowing your story to become a testimony.

There are parts of this story that are difficult because they are real. But there are also parts that are beautiful because they show what redemption can look like.

Your life reminded me that the person we see during the struggle is not necessarily the person who will remain there.

Thank you for being part of a story that reminds us that restoration is possible.

TO THOSE WHO HAVE STRUGGLED

To everyone who has fought a battle that other people could not see, your life matters.

Your story matters even when you do not yet know how it will end.

Your recovery matters.

Your return matters.

Your decision to keep going matters.

You are not simply the worst thing you have ever done.

You are still a person worth loving.

TO THOSE WHO PRAYED

To everyone who has prayed for someone they love while wondering whether anything was changing, thank you for continuing to pray.

Sometimes we pray without knowing what God is doing.

Sometimes we pray through tears.

Sometimes we pray when we have no words left.

But prayer can continue even when circumstances have not changed yet.

What looks like the end of a story may only be the place where God begins writing something new.

ABOVE ALL

Above all, I give thanks to God.

He is the One who sees what people cannot see.

He is the One who knows what is underneath the brokenness.

He is the One who can reach into places that human hands cannot reach.

He is the One who can restore what seemed beyond restoration.

He sees the worshipper underneath the struggle.

He sees the person underneath the label.

He sees the future when we can only see the past.

To everyone who has played a part in this story, whether you know it or not, thank you.

Some of you may recognize yourselves in these pages.

Others may simply recognize the struggle.

My hope is that when you reach the final page, you see what I have come to see.

Grace is still possible.

Bradley Hobbs
