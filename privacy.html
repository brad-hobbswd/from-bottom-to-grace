Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you visit this website.

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit the From the Bottom to Grace website.

The website is operated in connection with the book From the Bottom to Grace by Bradley Hobbs.

Information You Provide

If you contact the author through this website, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, email address, and the contents of your message.

This information is used only for the purpose for which you provide it, such as responding to your message.

Information Collected Automatically

Like many websites, this website may be hosted through a third party that automatically processes limited technical information needed to deliver the website.

This may include information such as browser type, device type, approximate geographic information, referring pages, and basic server or security information.

The website itself does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information simply because you visit a page.

Cookies

This website may use cookies or similar technologies if they are provided by the hosting provider, embedded services, analytics services, or other technologies used on the site.

You can generally control cookies through your browser or device settings.

Third Party Services

Certain features may rely on third party services, such as website hosting, email delivery, analytics, payment processing, book retailers, or other external services.

Those services may have their own privacy policies governing how they process information.

Links to Other Websites

This website may contain links to websites operated by other organizations or companies.

Once you leave this website, the privacy practices of the external website apply. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any website you visit.

Children's Privacy

This website is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children.

Data Security

Reasonable measures may be used to protect information submitted through this website. However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

If you contact the author and later have questions about the information you provided, you may contact the author using the contact information provided on the website.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as the website develops or as applicable requirements change.

The updated date at the beginning of this policy indicates when the policy was most recently revised.

Your privacy matters.
